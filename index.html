A LITTLE CONTEXT

Curious by design.

I started being interested in level design since I was a kid, attracted by how the games lead the players to a goal while making the experience fun. I allways stayed hours reserching in every space, looking fr secrets or just engoing the world that was in front of my eyes. Once I grew up I started to feel curious of how games where done and if in a future I could create experiences that make feel the same feelings I had as a kid.
