I'm Andreu Miró, a game design graduate interested in building clear, engaging and memorable player experiences.

A LITTLE CONTEXT

Curious by design.

My interest in level design began when I was a child. I was fascinated by how games guided players toward their goals while keeping the experience enjoyable. I would spend hours exploring every corner, searching for secrets, or simply taking in the worlds around me. As I grew older, I became curious about how games were made and whether I could one day create experiences that inspired the same feelings I had as a child.
