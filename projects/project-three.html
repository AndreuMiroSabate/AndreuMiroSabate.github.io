TRIBALB

View Project ↗

01 / THE PROJECT

TribalB was my final degree project in Video Game Design and Development at CITM. I wanted to explore whether players could learn how to play a survival game without a conventional tutorial. My goal was to teach mechanics without written instructions, using the environment, sound, and visual effects—similar to how the first level of Super Mario Bros. introduces its mechanics.

02 / WHAT I DID

I began by researching tutorials in video games, particularly survival games, and the principles of level design. Based on this research, I started developing a prototype and designing the mechanics needed to support an environment-driven tutorial.

I followed a level-design workflow that began with gathering references, creating flow maps, and planning layouts for the game’s areas. I then built a blockout to test the mechanics before adding the final assets.

Once the prototype was complete, I invited people to play it and asked for feedback on their experience and whether they understood how to play.
