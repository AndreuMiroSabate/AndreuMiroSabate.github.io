GAME PROJECT / 001

Bound By Death.

View Project ↗

01 / THE PROJECT

Bound By Death is a local co-op, isometric 3D hack-and-slash game inspired by Diablo. It was developed using our own game engine by Ravenwhisp, a fictional game studio created as part of UPC’s master’s program in Advanced Programming for AAA Video Games. I worked alongside 17 teammates as Lead Level Designer and also contributed to QA, testing features in both the engine and the game.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

My primary responsibility was designing all three levels. This included planning their layouts and deciding where and how gameplay mechanics would be introduced. I also handled the process of bringing the levels into our engine, starting with blockouts and replacing the basic geometry with assets provided by our instructors. Due to limitations in our engine, I built the levels in Unity before exporting the completed layouts as .gltf files.

Once the levels were imported into our engine, I designed and implemented their lighting and shadows, along with post-processing effects such as fog and custom shaders created by my teammates.

As part of the QA team, I reviewed most of my teammates’ pull requests, tested new and updated features, and shared feedback based on my findings.

03 / REFLECTION

Working as Lead Level Designer meant taking on more responsibility than in my previous projects. It helped me improve my organizational and communication skills, as I needed to communicate decisions clearly and be someone my teammates could rely on for level-design-related questions.
