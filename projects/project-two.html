GAME PROJECT / 002

Alien Extraction

View Project ↗
01 / OVERVIEW

Alien Extraction is a Diablo-inspired game set in the Alien universe. It was developed using our own engine by Clapcom Studios, a fictional studio created for a university project during my Video Game Design and Development degree at CITM. I worked as a Level Designer and was also part of the QA team.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

As a Level Designer, I was responsible for designing the Base and Level 1. I drew layouts for both areas, created their blockouts, and integrated them into our engine. I also replaced the blockout geometry with final assets provided by our art department.

As part of the QA team, I tested the game during its final stages, identified bugs, reported issues, and suggested improvements to existing features.

We worked in two-week sprints, organizing the team into smaller Scrum groups. I occasionally took on the role of Scrum Master.

04 / WHAT I LEARN

This was my first experience working on a large team, and it taught me the importance of clear communication. Early in development, communication gaps between departments slowed our progress. As collaboration improved, our workflow became smoother and more efficient.
