GAME PROJECT / 002

Alien Extraction

View Project ↗
01 / OVERVIEW

Alien Extraction is a DIABLO-LIKE game themed in the ALIEN universe. This game was developed by Clapcom Studios, a fictional company created for a University project for my degree in video game design and development at CITM, with our own engine. In this project I work as a Level Designer and form part of the QA team.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

As a Level designer I was in charge of design the Base an the Level 1 of the game. To do it I draw the layout of the zones and made a blockout of the levels, to eventually add them to our own engine. Also I was in charge of change the geometry of the blockout for the final assets, given by our art department for each level of our game.

Regarding the QA team I was in charge of testing the game in the final stages, to see wich features can be improved and if there was any bug, report it.

In this project we worked with sprints of 2 weeks, and to make it work properly, we devided each sprint in small scrum groups, where sometimes I acted as a scrum master.

04 / WHAT I LEARN

This was my first project working with a large team. Thanks to that I learn the importance of the communication. At the beggining there was a lack of communication between departments, what made the progress of the project not as fast as we suposed. So once this communication improved, the work start being easier and faster.
